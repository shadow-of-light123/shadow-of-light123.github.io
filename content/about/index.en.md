## Why I Blog

For me, writing is one of the most effective ways to organize what I've learned.

A lot of things don't truly become yours the moment you "get it". Only when I reorganize, articulate, and write them down does the understanding really solidify. This blog is where I let that happen.

Over time, I'll be recording:

- Knowledge worth revisiting from my studies
- Problems and solutions from building projects
- Tools, methods, and lessons learned
- Thoughts on growth, expression, and long-term accumulation
